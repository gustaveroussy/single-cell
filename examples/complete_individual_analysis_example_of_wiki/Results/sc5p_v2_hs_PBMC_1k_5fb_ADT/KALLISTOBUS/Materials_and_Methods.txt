Reads quality control was performed using fastqc (version 0.12.1).
Reads were pseudo-mapped to the antibodies' tags, with kallisto (version 0.45.1) using its 'bus' subcommand and parameters corresponding to the 10X Chromium 5' scRNA-Seq v2 chemistry, and the unstranded parameter. The index was made with kallisto (version 0.45.1) with the k-mer parameter set to 15. Barcode correction using whitelist provided by the manufacturer (10X Genomics), sorting, and gene-based reads quantification were performed with BUStools (version 0.45.1).
