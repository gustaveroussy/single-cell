Reads quality control was performed using fastqc (version 0.12.1) and assignment to the expected genome species evaluated with fastq-screen (version 0.16.0).
Reads were pseudo-mapped to the Ensembl reference transcriptome v99 corresponding to the homo sapiens GRCH38 build, with kb-python (version 0.29.5) using its 'bus' subcommand and parameters corresponding to the 10X Chromium 5' scRNA-Seq v2 chemistry. The index was made with kb-python (version 0.29.5). Barcode correction using whitelist provided by the manufacturer (10X Genomics), sorting, and gene-based reads quantification were performed with BUStools (version 0.45.1).
